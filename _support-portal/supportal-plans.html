Lower-tier plan rule
No direct downgrade during an active paid cycle.
To move from Gold to Silver/Free, cancel Gold first. Gold remains fully active until Apr 14. At cycle end the account returns to the baseline plan; only then can a currently available lower-tier plan be purchased as a new subscription. There is no scheduled lower-tier activation.
Supportal Search ranking
Ranking is calculated automatically from the subscribed plan and accumulated rating. There is no manual bidding, ranking purchase screen, or separate ranking action.
Long-term commitment discount
A 6-month or 12-month commitment may receive an Admin-configured discount. The reference values in the specification are 10% for 6 months and 20% for 12 months. This discount does not stack with another concurrent discount; the higher discount applies alone.
