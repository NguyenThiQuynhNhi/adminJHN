Review time disclosure
Admin will review your booking within a maximum of 7 days from submission. If your chosen Start Date falls within the next 7 days, consider choosing a later date to ensure enough review time — any days lost to a late approval are not compensated or extended.
Payment: your default card is pre-authorized for the full locked package price at submission. It is charged once only if Admin approves. Rejection or review timeout releases the authorization. After approval, the booking cannot be cancelled and no refund is issued.
